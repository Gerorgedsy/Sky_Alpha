About the live data

Source: adsb.lol community ADS-B network. Public data is licensed under ODbL 1.0.

Refresh: This page checks for updates every 30 seconds. The shared backend caches the source for about 3½ minutes under the anonymous API allowance. The observation age is shown above the map.

Limits: Positions may be absent, incomplete, or delayed. A visible aircraft is not necessarily departing a New York airport. No live delay prediction is made from aircraft positions alone.
